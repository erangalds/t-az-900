# Azure Support Levels

Microsoft Azure offers four primary standalone support tiers: **Basic**, **Developer**, **Standard**, and **Professional Direct** (with **Unified Support** covering broad enterprise contracts).

---

### Support Tiers & Key Features

| Support Plan | Target Audience | Technical Support Access | Architecture & Advisory Support | Key Features Included |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Basic** | All Azure accounts (Free/Default) | **None** (Billing & subscription management only) | None | 24/7 self-help docs, Azure Advisor recommendations, Service Health dashboard, access to Microsoft Q&A forums. |
| **Developer** | Non-production / trial environments | Business hours only (via email/ticket submission) | General guidance based on best practices | Unlimited break-fix support tickets, access to Azure Advisor, prioritized responses on Microsoft Q&A. |
| **Standard** | Production workload environments | **24x7** access via phone and email | General architectural guidance | Unlimited break-fix tickets, third-party software troubleshooting support, high-severity escalation routing. |
| **Professional Direct (ProDirect)** | Business-critical workloads | **24x7** access via phone and email | Architectural guidance from a dedicated pool of **ProDirect Delivery Managers** | Proactive guidance, onboarding seminars, service reviews, incident escalation management, and access to Support APIs. |

---

### Service Level Agreements (Initial Response Times by Severity)

Azure defines technical issues across three severity levels:

* **Severity A:** Critical business impact (complete outage / severe loss of service)
* **Severity B:** Moderate business impact (work impaired, but workarounds exist)
* **Severity C:** Minimal business impact (minor inquiry / minor impediment)

| Plan | Severity A (Critical) | Severity B (Moderate) | Severity C (Minimal) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Basic** | N/A (No technical support) | N/A | N/A |
| **Developer** | Not Available (Sev A not supported) | Not Available (Sev B not supported) | **< 8 business hours** |
| **Standard** | **< 1 hour** (24/7) | **< 4 hours** | **< 8 business hours** |
| **Professional Direct** | **< 1 hour** (24/7) | **< 2 hours** | **< 4 business hours** |

*(Note: For enterprise mission-critical environments requiring faster turnarounds, Microsoft Unified / Rapid Response agreements offer Severity 1 responses within **15 minutes**).*

---

### Core Distinctions for Azure Certifications (AZ-900 / PL-900)

* **Billing and Subscription Support:** Included for **free** across **all** tiers (including Basic).
* **24x7 Technical Support:** Starts at the **Standard** tier (Developer is strictly business hours).
* **Severity A Tickets:** Can only be submitted under **Standard**, **Professional Direct**, or **Unified** (the Developer plan only permits Severity C).
* **Dedicated Advisory & Delivery Managers:** Exclusive to **Professional Direct** and **Unified**.
